# Customer Complaint Registration Portal

## Guide for Customers, Sales Team, and B2B Sales Channels

The Customer Complaint Registration Portal allows a customer or sales representative to submit a service request directly to Jacky's Service Center.

Use this link:

**https://service-jackys.github.io/complaints/**

The portal sends the submitted request to the Jacky's internal Service Portal, where the Customer Care Executive reviews the details and arranges the service appointment.

## When to use this portal

Use the portal when:

- A customer requires service support.
- An appliance or product has a fault or service issue.
- A B2B customer, school, branch, or corporate client requires assistance.
- A sales representative receives a service request and needs to forward it to Customer Care.
- A customer needs to provide product or complaint details before an appointment is arranged.

Please submit one complaint for each separate service requirement unless Customer Care instructs otherwise.

## Information to prepare

Please provide as much of the following information as possible:

- Customer or company name.
- Contact number.
- Customer email address, if available.
- Location or service address.
- Region or emirate.
- B2B branch, school, or site name, where applicable.
- Site contact person and contact number, where applicable.
- Customer number and sales order number, where available.
- Appliance brand and model.
- Serial number or item code.
- A clear description of the complaint.

The customer name, contact number, and complaint description are required. Other information can be completed or confirmed later by the Customer Care Executive.

## How to submit a complaint

1. Open **https://service-jackys.github.io/complaints/**.
2. Select the appropriate **Customer Type**:
   - B2C — Direct Customer.
   - B2B — Corporate Client.
   - B2B Sales Channel.
3. Enter the customer or company name.
4. Enter a reachable contact number.
5. Add the customer email, if available.
6. Enter the location and select the region.
7. Complete the B2B or site contact information when applicable.
8. Enter the brand, model, and serial number or item code if known.
9. Describe the fault or service requirement clearly.
10. Select **Submit Complaint**.

Include useful information in the complaint description, such as:

- What is not working.
- When the problem started.
- Any error code or unusual sound.
- Whether the appliance is completely non-functional.
- Any previous visit or service reference.
- The best time for Customer Care to contact the customer.

Do not enter appointment, technician, warranty, or internal status decisions in the complaint description. These are completed by the internal service team after review.

## Complaint reference number

After successful submission, the portal displays a complaint reference in this format:

```text
CMP-yymmdd-XXX
```

Example:

```text
CMP-260924-001
```

Keep the complaint reference number and share it with the customer or sales team for follow-up.

## What happens after submission

1. The complaint is received by Jacky's Customer Care team.
2. A Customer Care Executive reviews the submitted information.
3. Customer Care may contact the customer or sales representative if information is missing.
4. Customer Care completes the internal service details.
5. An appointment is created in the internal Service Portal.
6. The customer or sales representative is contacted with the appointment information.

Submitting a complaint does not itself confirm an appointment. The appointment is confirmed only after Customer Care creates and schedules it in the internal Service Portal.

## If information is incorrect

Keep the complaint reference number and contact Customer Care. Do not submit multiple duplicate complaints for the same issue unless instructed by the service team.

When contacting Customer Care, provide:

- Complaint reference number.
- Customer or company name.
- Contact number.
- A short explanation of the correction required.

## Important information

- Do not share internal Service Portal login details with customers.
- Do not send confidential customer information through public group chats.
- Ensure that the contact number is correct and reachable.
- Provide the full service location wherever possible.
- The request is subject to service-area coverage, product information, technician availability, warranty review, and Customer Care confirmation.
